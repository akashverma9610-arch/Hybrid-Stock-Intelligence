# Earnings acceleration and quarterly results

Quarterly Rule 24 reports deterministic reported growth only; it does not infer organic growth or one-off quality. Rule 25 exposes available YoY and QoQ revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and EPS changes instead of hiding them in one verdict. Rule 26 reports EBITDA-margin level and YoY change. QoQ compares sequential reported quarters; YoY compares the quarter four reported periods earlier, preserving seasonality context.

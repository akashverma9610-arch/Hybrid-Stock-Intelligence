# Company Sensitivity and Macro-to-Earnings Context

Company profiles are created only from structured source-derived, rule-derived, manually reviewed, or explicitly unknown attributes. They may describe debt/rates, FX/import/export, commodities, power, operating leverage, working capital, capex, demand, credit, inventory/capacity, customer/supplier, and policy sensitivity. A sector assumption is never silently substituted for a missing company fact.

`MacroToEarningsTransmissionEngine` produces bounded directional revenue, margin, cash-flow, and balance-sheet context with strength, horizon, confidence, warnings, model/profile version, as-of, and shared evidence lineage. It does not produce an EPS forecast or a final action. Company-specific pass-through and fixed-price qualifiers may yield materially different impact from the same sector event.

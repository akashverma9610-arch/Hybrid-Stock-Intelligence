## 2. Meaning and scientific limit of bootstrap

A bootstrap horizon is only a provisional measurement convention for a prospective paired-research endpoint. It is not calibrated, optimal, strategy truth, a production Gate-1 horizon, signal expiry, an actual holding-period mandate or an expected-return model parameter. It cannot generate a recommendation, price estimate or capital authorization.

Pre-specifying a study and separating planned analysis from exploration makes outcome-dependent choices more visible; it does not eliminate bias or guarantee valid findings. The Center for Open Science also stresses transparent changes and the limitations of already-observed data. This is methodological support for pre-registration, not financial evidence for a duration. [COS preregistration guidance](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg).

**HSI design inference:** a genuinely independent, evidenced, human-approved prior can make a prospective experiment interpretable even before paired calibration exists. An arbitrary number plus a timestamp does not satisfy that requirement. No value is chosen here, and no claim of empirical validation is made.

## 6. Human, RA and AI boundaries

An explicitly designated HSI human governance owner may review a documented research-only prior. Approval must bind the exact policy/value fingerprint, evidence and rationale, scope, limitations, approver/delegation, conflicts and real approval timestamp. A direct user-entered number is not that approval package. This task's authorization to create a governance document is not approval of any numeric seed.

An RA can supply review or act as an approver only if the HSI governance role is explicitly delegated and any relied-on registration claim is verified. No RA-specific prerequisite was found in the inspected **internal** HSI approval conventions. This is not a blanket legal exemption or a conclusion about future client-facing services.

SEBI's February 6, 2026 Master Circular addresses RA/entity responsibility when AI is used for client research services and requires supporting research records. An internal HSI approver ID does not establish regulatory status. Any future regulated distribution or service requires its own compliance assessment. [SEBI Master Circular, sections 1.7–1.8](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/feb-2026/1770375507051.pdf).

AI may gather sources, compare research, summarize rationale and check contradictions. AI may not choose the authoritative seed, approve policy, invent empirical support, authorize an arbitrary number, or rewrite a historical version. Human/RA approval does not make a seed calibrated or live-authoritative.

## 9. No retrospective grandfathering or outcome-dependent selection

A policy approved after original D cannot authorize that observation. An old policy ID or a later administrative replay timestamp cannot repair original D-time knowledge.

Retrospective exploration may be proposed only as **RETROSPECTIVE_EXPLORATORY_NON_AUTHORITATIVE**, distinctly separate from **PROSPECTIVE_GOVERNED_COHORT**. It may describe data feasibility, bias or design questions with disclosed prior data access. It may not invent decision events, become a governed cohort, select the best historical forward-return window or bypass later prospective validation. No exploratory run is authorized here.

Prohibited regardless of preregistration claims:

- retrospective duration optimization or best-return window scanning;
- choosing a seed after seeing a particular observation's outcome;
- changing it because early observations underperform;
- using the eventual calibration data to justify their own initial horizon;
- discarding negative outcomes or rewriting old endpoints;
- treating reused exploratory/OOS data as untouched validation.

An explicit source/integrity/legal stop can prevent future admission. It must retain the original denominator, exclusions and version history; it is not a way to hide poor outcomes or silently shorten a seed.

## 13. Bootstrap bias and lock-in

| Bias | Mechanism | Required later reporting/control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TRUNCATION | Events resolving outside the seed end are unobserved or differently measured. | Report endpoints, source coverage and the inability to infer unmeasured paths; do not extrapolate returns. |
| DURATION_CONDITIONING | The seed defines the estimand and sample maturity; resulting data are not horizon-neutral. | Report seed version and conditioning; a new duration needs its own prospective governance and validation. |
| CENSORING | Unresolved prices, suspension/delistings, late evidence and immature pairs selectively disappear if filtered. | Retain original denominator and all inherited exclusion/maturity states; predeclare treatment without zero/last-price filling. |
| STRATEGY_MISMATCH | One category may combine distinct strategies or both categorical horizons. | Pin exact strategy/category scope; report subgroup coverage; any later split applies prospectively, not outcome-based per pair. |
| REGIME_DEPENDENCE | Early observations may represent one market environment. | Report D-known regime/subperiod/sector coverage and uncertainty; no stationarity claim or invented adequacy threshold. |
| SURVIVOR_BIAS | Current membership or complete-price/winner-only analysis omits failed origins. | Archive original identity/universe/parcel context and missing outcomes; no synthetic backfill or dropping inconvenient origins. |
| OUTSIDE_SEED_BLINDNESS | Official endpoints alone cannot evaluate resolution beyond that duration. | State limitation; separately governed raw-path retention may inform later research but never supplies a replacement official horizon. |
| ADAPTIVE_REVIEW | Changing the seed after early underperformance contaminates later evaluation and erases failure evidence. | Preserve every version and prior data access; independently approved prospective successor and untouched validation required, never same-cohort relabeling. |

The first dataset is seed-conditioned, not neutral. Report all original origins, unresolved/censored states, strategy/regime/sector coverage, source limitations and prior data exposure. No sample minimum, confidence threshold, review cadence or stability acceptance number is invented.

Later governed review can validate a seed for a stated research scope, replace it, split future applicability by category/strategy, or reject it. Existing cohorts always retain their original version and endpoint. No permanent acceptance by inertia or rewriting outcomes under a better-looking successor.

## 15. Prospective-first result and transition

The conditionally supportable path is a **new prospective cohort** after explicit approval and registration of a complete independently justified package and closure of its source prerequisites. It preserves PIT by refusing old pre-policy D records. It cannot currently begin.

Time to evidence is not numerically estimable: it depends on a yet-unapproved horizon, actual completed endpoints/source availability, genuine event arrival and later governed adequacy/OOS requirements.

The future stages remain distinct:

`research bootstrap -> evidence accumulation -> calibration -> OOS/walk-forward validation -> policy proposal -> independent approval -> separate possible production authority`.

No stage automatically promotes to the next. A new mapping inferred from seed data must face separately governed untouched prospective validation. Neither an RA signature, successful software tests nor a mature cohort bypasses this sequence.

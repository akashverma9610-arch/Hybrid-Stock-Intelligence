## Reuse decision

No new Long-Term Evidence Dossier contract or storage is created. The approved
Core V1 `BrainOutputProjection` for `DEEP_ANALYSIS`, composed only from an
existing validated `BrainOutputEvaluationContext`, is the non-duplicative
read-only dossier boundary. It retains deterministic projection identity and
fingerprint; canonical instrument; timezone-aware as-of; unified-state and
evaluation-context lineage; immutable evidence-domain references; availability,
freshness, warnings and blockers; stable ordering; and a no-action surface.

Its `COMPANY_RESEARCH`, `RELIABILITY_INTEGRITY`, `RESEARCH_DIAGNOSTIC`,
`MARKET_CONTEXT`, `SECTOR_CONTEXT`, and `NEWS_CATALYST` sections are the
eligible existing sources for future long-term consumers. Rule 33 and Rule 46
remain part of `RESEARCH_DIAGNOSTIC` lineage exactly as existing canonical
diagnostics; they are not reinterpreted or copied into a second thesis aggregate.

This reuse has no new API/UI/product activation. It does not classify Quality
at Fair Value or Compounder, create valuation/scenarios, access a portfolio,
or produce ADD/HOLD/TRIM/EXIT/BUY/SELL/ROTATE vocabulary.

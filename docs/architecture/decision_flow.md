# Decision Flow

Existing canonical diagnostics create eligible/PARTIAL diagnostic results from
frozen snapshots. The existing scorecard applies its versioned Decision Score
policy; confidence remains a separate result. The six hard gates consume their
own frozen dependencies. Portfolio context and the existing capital-rotation
engine then produce analytical comparisons. Risk and execution contracts may
form non-executable signal/paper artifacts only under their existing rules.

No diagnostic wrapper is a score, no score bypasses gates, no gate is an order,
and no rotation result mutates a holding. Current market integrity engines feed
context/evidence only: Crisis is not a liquidation instruction and euphoria is
not a market-top call.

Economic and catalyst intelligence may provide structured research context, fundamental/earnings outlook, sector context, and completeness warnings. It may not calculate a second score, directly emit BUY/SELL, or alter the final engine.

Pre-breakout and inflection candidate engines operate before canonical research.
Their `SetupQuality` is a strategy-fit measure, separate from Decision Score and
confidence. A candidate may subsequently be WATCH, NO_TRADE, or
RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE under the existing Brain; candidate generation is not an
execution or rotation priority.

# Economic and News Intelligence

Economic intelligence is an evidence-first context layer:

`Macro Intelligence → Economic Regime → Sector/Micro Transmission → Company Sensitivity → News/Catalyst Validation → Forecast/Risk/Evidence Impact → Existing Decision Engine`

It has no Macro Score, News Score, direct BUY/SELL rule, automatic promotion, or trading behaviour. All inputs preserve source hierarchy, as-of availability, freshness, reliability, confidence, warnings, and internal evidence lineage.

The supported input-family boundaries are monetary/rates, inflation, growth/fiscal, FX/external, flows, credit, rural, power, global macro, earnings revision breadth, inventory/capacity cycles, rating migration, and policy implementation. Missing source coverage remains unavailable or research incomplete; no web scraping or invented facts are used.

The same fact may be present in macro, sector, company, and catalyst contexts, but its common evidence ID is retained as one lineage. It cannot become several independent confirmations.

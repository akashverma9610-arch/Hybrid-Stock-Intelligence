# Brain Contract

The system preserves the chain **FACT → DATA → INTERPRETATION → INFERENCE**.
Facts have source/provenance and timestamps; data is normalized and validated;
interpretations are deterministic engine results; inferences remain qualified.

- Missing data is neither neutral, zero, nor pass.
- Point-in-time access, historical membership, and lifecycle eligibility are
  explicit; no look-ahead or survivorship assumption is permitted.
- API/live mode never silently uses mock data and agents never manufacture facts.
- Analytical endpoints never hide execution, and diagnostic count never creates
  a Decision Score.
- Decision Score, confidence, six gates, and rotation are independent contracts.
- Cash is a valid allocation; euphoria is not a top and crisis is not sell-all.
- SMC, volume, and institutional behaviour are proxy evidence only.
- Macro, sector transmission, company sensitivity, and catalyst evidence are contextual; they cannot create a Macro/News Score or direct action.
- Republished news keeps its original event timing; article count is not catalyst confidence.
- Self-improvement cannot promote, approve, activate, or deploy a strategy.
- Partial coverage cannot support a "best NSE opportunity" assertion.

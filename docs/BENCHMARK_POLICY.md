# Canonical Benchmark Policy

Registry version: `benchmark-registry-v1`. Definitions only; calculations are not implemented.

Canonical benchmarks: Nifty 50 buy-and-hold, Nifty 500, relevant sector indices, simple momentum, simple moving-average strategy, randomized stock selection, previous NSE Stock Manager strategy version, and trader-like baseline.

Canonical comparison metrics: total return, CAGR, max drawdown, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, expectancy, profit factor, win rate, average R multiple, turnover, transaction/friction costs, capital efficiency, rotation efficiency, market-regime performance, sector performance, and out-of-sample performance.

Any future implementation must retain benchmark universe, date range, rebalancing, cost/fill assumptions, data version, and strategy version to prevent misleading comparisons.

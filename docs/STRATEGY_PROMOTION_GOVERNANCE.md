# Strategy Promotion Governance

`CandidateSelectionResult` is experimental evidence only. A promotion packages immutable OOS, robustness, benchmark, policy, and baseline references into a proposal. Frozen policy eligibility can make the package eligible for human review, but cannot approve it.

Only an explicit human `APPROVE` review may create a new immutable approved strategy version. Missing, insufficient, failed-audit, risk, robustness, complexity, or benchmark evidence blocks eligibility. A proposal becomes stale for new approval when its recorded baseline no longer matches the active approved strategy; its historical record remains unchanged.

No service in this system promotes or activates a strategy automatically.

Future OOS assignments and their execution results are evidence packages only. Even positive TEST results may not manufacture a promotion proposal, review, approved version, or activation.
# Experiment isolation

A human-approved hypothesis may create a controlled experiment draft only. It does not create any promotion proposal, eligibility result, promotion review, approved version, or activation; promotion governance remains a separate human-controlled chain.
